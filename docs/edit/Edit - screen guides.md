# Screen guides

Status: idea. Not part of the viewer.

Guides are lines drawn over the picture in edit mode. They are not adjustments. They are not written into the adj file or the JPEG, and they do not change the histogram. One button cycles them. Each click advances, and only one guide is drawn:

1. None
2. Thirds
3. Center
4. Pointer

The click after Pointer returns to None. Edit mode opens on None. The button's label is the step that is showing.

A line is one pixel on the screen. Zoom does not thicken it. Pan and zoom move the picture under the lines that are tied to the frame.

## Thirds

Two vertical lines and two horizontal lines. They sit at one third and two thirds of the frame, so they split the framed picture into three equal bands each way. The frame is the bright rectangle, the picture that will be saved. The dimmed margin is outside these lines.

## Center

One vertical line and one horizontal line through the middle of that same frame. They are the reference for a centered subject and for a horizon that should pass through the middle.

## Pointer

One vertical line and one horizontal line through the pointer. They stay horizontal and vertical on the screen. The horizon angle does not rotate them, which is what makes a tilted roof or a tilted horizon visible against them.

They run across the whole photo, including the dimmed margin, so an edge that is about to be cropped can still be checked. They are hidden while the pointer is outside the photo.
